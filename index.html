Law Firm

Web platformClient and Lawyer roles12 features

A web-based legal service platform that connects clients with qualified lawyers. Clients search for a lawyer by specialization, location, experience and fee, then hire them for a case. Ratings, reviews and a leaderboard help clients choose with confidence, which makes legal help easier to find and trust.

  • User authenticationRegister and log in as a Client or a Lawyer, with a dashboard for each role.
  • Lawyer searchFilter by specialization, location, years of experience and consultation fee.
  • Case requestsClients send a request, lawyers accept or reject it, and the case status updates.
  • Ratings and reviewsAfter a case closes, clients leave 1 to 5 stars and a written review. Averages update automatically.
  • LeaderboardTop lawyers ranked by average rating and completed cases.
  • Payments and billingInvoices after acceptance, full or installment payments, history and outstanding balance.
  • Document managementContracts, evidence and court notices stored against the right case, shared with authorized users.
  • AppointmentsBook available time slots. Lawyers accept, reject or reschedule, and overlaps are prevented.
  • Admin and verificationAdmins check credentials. Only verified lawyers can offer services, and the approver and date are recorded.
  • In-app messagingSecure chat organized by case, so every conversation stays with its case.
  • NotificationsAlerts for requests, appointments, messages, uploads, payments and verification.
  • Case statusClients always know where their case stands.

Seven-Segment Display

Digital Logic DesignGroup of 4Gate limits

A seven-segment display system that can show every character in the phrase JUStICELEAGUE. My three classmates and I designed it for our Digital Logic Design course. It meant working with Boolean logic, character encoding and circuit simplification, all within strict limits on how many gates we could use. Thanks to my teammates for the teamwork and effort.
